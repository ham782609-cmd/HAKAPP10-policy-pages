Privacy policy

Effective: November 15, 2026

Information stored on your device

Alarm times, days, missions, answer languages, wake-confirmation preferences, app language, cached questions and session questions, answers, identifiers and scheduling/cleanup state are stored on your device. They support alarms, consistent missions and recovery. The app has no feature that uploads these settings or sessions to our server. There is currently no account registration.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone and speech permissions are used to recognize mission answers. Regular voice missions request on-device recognition; unsupported languages are unavailable. Allowing online recognition in experimental tools may send audio to Apple for processing. The app does not save recording files or send audio or transcripts to our server. Transcripts are used for display and answer matching.

Question sync and updates

Builds connected to the question bank read public questions from Supabase and cache them on your device. These requests do not include speech or alarm settings. Connection information such as IP addresses and request times may be processed in service-provider logs. Builds containing the Shorebird updater process app, release and patch information, platform and CPU details, a random installation identifier and update events to check, download and report updates.

Diagnostics and support

Device diagnostics may contain alarm/session identifiers, scheduling, stop and cancellation results and error codes. Tests with separately enabled developer speech diagnostics may log transcripts to the Mac console. If you contact us, we use your email address and the content or attachments you choose to send to answer and resolve the issue. Opening support or copying our address does not send logs or audio.

Retention, deletion and permissions

You can delete alarm settings in the app. Session records may remain locally after completion or ending to support recovery and cancel late followups. Removing the app removes its local data; restoring an OS backup may restore it. End alarms and wake-confirmation sessions before removal. Manage microphone, speech and alarm permissions in OS settings. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us.

External services

Apple provides speech recognition, Supabase supplies questions and Shorebird supplies updates in builds that include it. Online processing and service-log retention follow each provider’s policies and settings; processing may take place outside your country. The app does not request location or contacts permissions and has no advertising-tracking feature. Provider policies are listed below.

Operator and contact

HAK provides Rise & Reply. Find our contact address under Contact support for questions about the app or your privacy.

Changes

When features or data handling change, we update the in-app information and the document’s effective date. Changes requiring additional permission or consent are explained before use of that feature. Contact support with any questions.
