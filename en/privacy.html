Privacy policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

Information on your device

No account is required. Alarm settings, app preferences and mission progress are stored on your device to provide the app’s features. They are not sent to our servers.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone and speech permissions are used to check mission answers. Speech is recognized on your device. The app does not save audio recordings or send your voice or recognized text to us.

Information processed during use

External services process a random installation identifier, app version, and update activity and error information to deliver updates and keep the service working reliably. Connection information such as your IP address may also be processed when connecting to the internet. This information is not used for advertising tracking.

Contacting us

You can write an inquiry in the app and send it yourself through your mail app. If you contact us, we use your email address and the content you send to respond. Opening the form or writing a draft does not send it.

Deletion and permissions

You can delete saved alarms in the app. Removing the app removes its local data. Manage alarm, microphone and speech permissions in iOS Settings. We use inquiry emails and the content you send to respond and follow up, then delete them from our mailbox within 90 days after the inquiry is resolved. If retention is required by law, the applicable period applies.
