The service

Rise & Reply helps you wake with scheduled alarms and voice missions. Missions, languages and alarm features depend on the app version, operating system, device support and permissions. Experimental tools are for testing and may behave differently from regular alarms.

Alarm and speech limitations

Check the time and permissions, then test your alarm. Power loss, app removal, system interruptions, permission changes and speech errors can prevent normal operation. System controls can stop alarms; the app does not guarantee prevention of dismissal, recording while locked or successful waking. Use an additional alarm for important commitments.

Wake confirmation and your controls

Wake confirmation applies only when you turn it on for an alarm. Stopping it through the system schedules up to three followups, 60 seconds apart after each stop. A confirmed answer triggers cleanup of remaining followups; cancellation failures may require a cleanup retry. You can turn the setting off or end a session. Check the app and system alarm state to confirm the result.
